================================================================
OPTIMAL TILT — Jakarta, Indonesia (lat -6.21°)
================================================================

Annual fixed tilt : 10° (face North)
Summer tilt       : 0°
Winter tilt       : 21.2°
Est. gain vs flat : 2.2% (rough rule of thumb)

Within ~15° of the equator the gain is small; a 10–15° tilt is
still recommended so rain washes dust off the panels.
